      What is an Internet speed test?

      An Internet speed test measures how quickly data moves between your device and a test server. SpeedGauge runs real transfers in your browser and reports download speed, upload speed, ping and jitter.

      What does download speed mean?

      Download speed is how fast data arrives on your device, in megabits per second (Mbps). It matters for streaming, web pages, app updates and file downloads.

      What does upload speed mean?

      Upload speed is how fast your device sends data out. It matters for video calls, cloud backups, sending large files and live streaming.

      What is ping?

      Ping (latency) is the time a small request takes to reach a server and return, in milliseconds. Lower ping feels more responsive in games and calls. SpeedGauge takes several samples and shows the median.

      What is jitter?

      Jitter describes how much your ping varies from one sample to the next. SpeedGauge shows the median deviation of its ping samples from their median — a practical stability indicator, not a carrier-grade measurement.

      Why can speed results change?

      Browser-based results can vary because of Wi-Fi conditions, mobile signal, device hardware, browser behaviour, distance to the test server, Internet congestion, routing, server capacity and background traffic. Your ISP plan speed and your measured throughput are not the same thing: a 500 Mbps plan may measure lower over Wi-Fi or a busy route.

      How to get a cleaner speed test

      • Pause other downloads, uploads and video streaming.
      • Turn off VPN or proxy tools while testing.
      • Move closer to your router, or use a cable if possible.
      • Keep this tab open and in the foreground until the test finishes.
      • Run the test a few times and compare results.

      Wi-Fi and mobile Internet speed testing

      The same test works on Wi-Fi, Ethernet and mobile data. On mobile data, note the “Data tested” figure on the result screen — a test transfers real data, so it counts toward your plan.

      About SpeedGauge

      SpeedGauge is a browser-based Internet performance testing tool designed to provide a quick view of download speed, upload speed, ping and jitter. Results may vary depending on your device, Wi-Fi/mobile signal, server distance, congestion, routing and browser limitations. It does not claim laboratory accuracy and reports observed throughput between your browser and the selected test server.

      Privacy

      SpeedGauge does not need your personal information to perform a browser-based speed test. It asks for no camera, microphone or location access, contains no advertising scripts, and keeps your history only in this browser’s local storage.
